# Review and contribution policy

This repository is a selected research-source publication. This portfolio candidate does not authorize an open contribution program, public data submissions or changes to scientific methods.

Researchers may examine the released source within the [license](LICENSE.md). Before proposing new code, datasets, figures or claims, contact the project owner through the [academic website](https://ichsan.ibios.org) and agree the scope and rights review. Do not upload patient information, credentials, unpublished results or third-party material whose redistribution rights are unclear.

Do not interpret the repository's existing issue setting as approval to submit sensitive material. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Any approved contribution must include attribution, source provenance, bounded evidence and relevant tests; it must not silently weaken scientific caveats or the selective-release boundary.
