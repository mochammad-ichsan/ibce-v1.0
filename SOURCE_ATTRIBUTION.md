# Source-use notices for review

This repository candidate contains **code only**, not data from these sources. Their terms do not license IBCE's own source code. Confirm applicability and the code owner's approval before publication, and review terms again before adding any data or imagery.

- **Human Protein Atlas (HPA):** HPA states that copyrightable parts of its database are CC BY 4.0, but warns that third-party data may have additional constraints. Its citation guidelines require a primary publication plus the HPA website for general use, and direct links and credit for specific images or data. [Official licence and citation guidance](https://www.proteinatlas.org/about/licence).
- **NCI Genomic Data Commons (GDC) / TCGA:** GDC's open-data policy requires users not to attempt re-identification of research participants and to acknowledge the datasets and repository in disclosures or publications. Controlled-access data needs separate authorization. [Official data access policies](https://gdc.cancer.gov/access-data/data-access-policies).
- **Cellosaurus:** Its FAQ identifies CC BY 4.0, requiring attribution, a license link, and indication of changes. The original curated snapshot records are intentionally excluded here. [Official FAQ, question 22](https://www.cellosaurus.org/faq#Q22).

No data redistribution permission is inferred from these notices. Specific input files and any third-party material they contain must be reviewed separately if a future release includes them.
