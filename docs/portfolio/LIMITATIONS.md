# Research limitations and unsupported interpretations

## Methods

Median splitting discards continuous expression information and is not a validated biological threshold. Ties can produce unequal groups. The unadjusted log-rank comparison is not a covariate-adjusted model and does not correct for age, subtype, treatment, stage, cohort selection, multiple testing or other confounding. Minimum-case and event-count gates do not establish statistical power.

Kaplan-Meier curves are descriptive. Their use does not demonstrate causal effects, individual prognosis, clinical prediction or a treatment recommendation. A missing/withheld p-value is not zero and is not evidence of equivalence.

## Data

Input provenance, case matching, censoring/follow-up quality, missingness, duplicated measurements and sample selection affect results. Keeping nTPM/pTPM labels does not independently verify the source metric. Protein staining, RNA measurements, cell-line evidence and patient outcomes are distinct modalities. Cell-line findings do not automatically generalize to patients.

The public repository distributes no biological dataset. Published table definitions are not data. Imported HPA prognosis labels and IBCE's exploratory survival calculations are separate products, not necessarily independent corroboration.

## Validation and reproducibility

The full application depends on excluded host infrastructure and is not independently executable from this snapshot. The synthetic demonstration reproduces only selected pure computations on generated inputs. Passing software tests does not validate data ingestion, every gene cohort, statistical study design, biological mechanisms or clinical use.

Historical reference-comparison claims remain labeled project reports unless their supporting execution records are supplied. Manuscript acceptance, publication, indexing and external peer review are not established by repository metadata.

## AI and attribution

AI-generated explanations and translations can be incorrect and require source checking. The repository does not quantify the researcher's manual coding contribution. Confirm individual scientific and engineering roles before public dissemination.

## Required future research

Independent data-quality review; justified cohort/censoring assumptions; prespecified hypotheses; confounder and multiplicity handling; reproducible dataset-version records; external validation; and, only if clinical claims are later pursued, appropriately governed prospective evaluation. These are proposed requirements, not completed work.
